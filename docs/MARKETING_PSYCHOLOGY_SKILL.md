# SKILL-MKT-001 — Marketing Psychology & Consumer Insight

## Purpose
Turn consumer psychology and psychographic insights into governed, testable marketing hypotheses for Luna Vine Apparel. The skill may recommend positioning, messaging, offer framing, pricing tests, product-page improvements, lifecycle messaging, and campaign hypotheses. It may not declare a persuasion theory to be customer truth without evidence.

## Council
1. Consumer Psychologist — motivation, cognition, decision behavior.
2. Premium Brand Strategist — protects quiet-luxury positioning and Luna Vine brand codes.
3. Growth/CRO Specialist — conversion and experiment quality.
4. Customer Voice Analyst — grounds assumptions in reviews, interviews, search, support and behavior data.
5. Adversarial Reviewer — challenges causal claims, unsupported inference and unintended effects.
6. Ethics & Compliance Reviewer — blocks dark patterns, deceptive scarcity, fabricated social proof, hidden consent and exploitative targeting.

## Workflow
Psychographic Segment → Customer Evidence → Hypothesis → Behavioral Models Considered → Council Review → Brand/Ethics Gates → Legal/Claims/Cultural gates when applicable → Experiment Draft → Approval → Run → Results → Learning Registry.

## Hard controls
- Psychology insight is a hypothesis until measured evidence supports it.
- Ethics and Brand reviews are mandatory for every hypothesis.
- Legal, Claims, and Cultural reviews are dynamically required when applicable.
- Experiment creation is blocked unless all applicable reviews PASS.
- Fake scarcity and fake social proof are prohibited.
- Reference prices and urgency claims require evidence.
- No pre-checked marketing consent or hidden continuity.
- No protected-attribute targeting or exploitative vulnerability targeting without approved policy/legal basis.
- Revenue alone never determines success; contribution margin and guardrail metrics must be considered.
- Reusable organizational learning requires recorded experiment evidence and approval.

## Core records
- `marketing_psych_models`: governed behavioral model registry + ethical boundaries.
- `psychographic_segments`: values, desires, frustrations, triggers, objections, tone and evidence quality.
- `marketing_hypotheses`: problem, intervention, models, metric, guardrails and gate statuses.
- `marketing_council_reviews`: reviewer verdict, score, rationale and risk flags.
- `marketing_experiments`: control/variant, audience, channel, metrics and result.
- `marketing_learning_registry`: validated reusable learning.
- `marketing_skill_runs`: trace of AI-assisted executions and model/prompt version when enabled.

## Initial behavioral model registry
The migration seeds common behavioral and decision models including identity-based motivation, self-consistency, social proof, authority, reciprocity, commitment, framing, anchoring, loss aversion, scarcity, defaults, choice overload, decoy/contrast effects, processing fluency, mere exposure, peak-end, goal-gradient, present bias, endowment, narrative transportation, Jobs-to-be-Done, reactance and value-first exchange. Each includes an explicit ethical boundary.
